# MedShare
An expiry-aware medicine donation and redistribution platform. MedShare connects individuals with unused, unexpired medicines to NGOs, clinics, and verified recipients who need them — using a First-Expiry-First-Out (FEFO) matching engine to minimize medical wastage and improve access.

# Problem
A significant share of medicines purchased by households in India go unused and expire — due to incomplete courses, discontinued prescriptions, or dosage changes. Meanwhile, NGOs, small clinics, and economically weaker patients often can't afford essential medicines. There is no organized digital channel connecting this surplus to the people who need it.

# Solution
MedShare lets donors list unused, unexpired medicines with batch and expiry details. NGOs and verified recipients can search and request what they need. The core of the platform is a FEFO (First-Expiry-First-Out) matching engine — a real inventory-management principle used in pharmacies — which prioritizes soon-to-expire donations first when fulfilling a request, ensuring stock is used before it's wasted.
