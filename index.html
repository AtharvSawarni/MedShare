How MedShare Works

A secure and compliant path from your cabinet to those who need it most.

1. List Unused Medicine

Input basic details, batch numbers, and unexpired dates. Upload a photo for primary verification.

2. NGOs Verify & Collect

Partner NGOs perform a strict standard inspection check and approve or coordinate safe collection protocols.

3. Delivered to Patients

Verified, essential medications are distributed to people in need or clinical patients who cannot afford treatment.
